Why Tallow?

  • Tallow is rich in fat-soluble vitamins like A, D, E, and K, which penetrates deep into the skin.
  • Tallow closely mimics human sebum, making it easily absorbed and less likely to clog pores.
  • Tallow has natural antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects, which can help sooth eczema, psoriasis, and other irritated skin conditions.
  • Free of harsh additives, which makes it a great option for sensitive skin.
