About Us.

"The earth is the Lord's, and everything in it." (Psalm 24:1)

Stewarding Today. We're a first-generation farming family, called to a simple life of good stewardship and building something that lasts. Our journey into natural body care began after the birth of our first baby, when we went looking for something gentle and truly natural for her skin, and felt led to make it ourselves instead.

What started as a small batch for our own family has grown, by God's provision, into Faithful Sower Farmers. Every product begins with locally sourced, grass-fed beef suet, and we make each batch by hand with natural ingredients we can trust, nothing you can't pronounce, nothing your skin doesn't need.

Around our place, stewardship looks like chickens for eggs, a pig, two goats, and a garden that feeds our family through the seasons. We're still growing where God is leading us, next year we're planning to move onto a new piece of land to keep building the kind of farm we've prayed for. It's a lot of work, but we trust it's the kind worth doing.

We believe good things take time, faith, and honest ingredients. That's true for how we farm, and it's true for everything we make. Our prayer is that this stays a family business for generations, growing a little each year, the same slow, faithful way everything else here does. Sowing for generations to come. — Reuben and Kasey Nehrt
